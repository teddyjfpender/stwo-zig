## Completion judgment and limits

No new boundary or final-receiving logic hole was identified in the inspected path. The implementation has source-level links from independent initialization and expected output through RAM/CPU closure, recursive public supply, exact upper coverage and final proof verification. The actual retained run is nevertheless **pending**: prior tiny fixture roots and current progress do not establish a completed 139M block root. Publication requires the terminal window, all folds and the final serialized `verifyFull` call to finish, followed by artifact/hash and phase/peak-memory records. This note does not infer any of those events from progress or report flags.

The final receiver currently needs actual ELF/input, the full checkpoint catalog’s complete intermediate RAM snapshots (about 2.7 GiB), and thin per-window metadata to reconstruct statement-dependent fixed keys. Intermediate checkpoints may be supplied untrusted: their IDs are recomputed and their RAM boundaries/adjacency are proved. They are not trusted execution-oracle facts. This is independent proof verification with large auxiliary receiving data, **not** a universal fixed-VK or bounded-I/O-only verifier. No privacy claim for published receiving snapshots is made. See `full-root-public-checkpoint-soundness-v1.md` and `bounded-full-block-receiving-v2.md` for the precise dependency and a future block-global RAM route.

This is a completion-path audit, not a new formal verification of all primitive AIR/FRI/hash implementations. No timing extrapolation, Cairo-wrapper completion, admission-cache qualification, or full-run result is asserted.
