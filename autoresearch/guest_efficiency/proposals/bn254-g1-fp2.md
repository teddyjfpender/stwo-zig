# Proposal: BN254 affine G1 add/double and Fp2 multiply services

Status: **candidate, measured; pays on every block that calls a BN254 precompile.
Recommended after the SHA-256, bulk-memory and arith256_mod services.**
Execution-only evidence; not promotable.

## Motivation (measured, evmone client, 15-block basket, 2026-11-01)

Reference: the evmone client with the candidate memory, SHA-256 and arith256_mod
services (`--candidate-set mem,sha,arith`). There evmone's BN254 code already
multiplies through arith256_mod (patch 0004). That still leaves 0.30
instructions per gas: Jacobian point formulas, Fq2 Karatsuba glue, 47-instruction
arith256_mod packet copies and to-affine inversions.

| scored | 5.392 | -4.63% | 75,692 | -4.12% / -4.00% / -3.81% |
| holdout | 4.704 | -2.90% | 65,369 | -2.53% nominal |
| all | 5.256 | -4.33% | 73,654 | -3.75% nominal |

All guards pass with exact outputs. Mix-adjusted (ALU vs memory cells): -4.65%.

- The services used 10,752 adds, 37,005 doubles and 133,776 Fq2 products.
  arith256_mod calls fell from 794,845 to 49,058, and 105.9M instructions were
  saved.
- The BN254 bucket fell from 0.300 to 0.064 instr/gas.

## ABI

Tags `0x0003_0100` (add), `0x0003_0101` (double), `0x0003_0102` (Fp2 mul):
pointer packets, affine plain-form coordinates, preconditions `x_p ≠ x_q` (add)
and `y ≠ 0` (double). See `notes/candidate-curves-abi.md`. The glue
(`client_evmone/glue/curves_stwo.cpp`, patch 0020):

- ecMul: reduces the scalar mod r and uses a 4-bit fixed window, about 252
  doubles and 64 adds.
- ecAdd: one service call, after handling infinity, `P = Q` and `P = -Q`.
- ecPairing: the tower's Fq2 `multiply`/`sqr` go to `bn254_fp2_mul`.

## AIR sketch

A ZisK-ArithEq-style product chip over byte limbs, the same shape as the
admitted secp product row:

- **Add:** three relations, `λ(x2 - x1) = y2 - y1`, `x3 = λ² - x1 - x2` and
  `y3 = λ(x1 - x3) - y1`, each `Σ products + linear = q·p`.
- **Double:** `2λy = 3x²`, plus the `x3` and `y3` relations.
- **Fp2 mul:** two two-product relations.

Required for soundness:

- `x1 ≠ x2` (and `y ≠ 0`) by a chunk-difference inverse. Otherwise λ is free
  when the guest passes equal points.
- Canonical outputs (`x3, y3 < p`) by borrow chains.
- Range checks on λ, the quotients and the carries.

The operands come through the word16 bridge and closed RAM via pointer words.
Estimated 10,100 / 8,200 / 9,600 cells per call (`candidate-curve-costs.md`).

## Expected effect

On the evmone client, about -4.3% instructions and -3.8% committed bits over
all blocks. On blocks heavy in BN254 precompiles (zk-proof verifiers), -7% to
-13%. A GLV split of ecMul would cut about 100 service calls per ecMul. That is
worth about 0.03% here and not pursued.

## Prover work

- Truth AIR (product chip with a per-op selector, shareable with the arith256 /
  secp chips).
- Pointer-packet bridge (new: operands addressed through the packet).
- Services lane and receiver.
- Recursion support.
- Adversarial tests: equal x, y = 0, non-canonical inputs, off-curve inputs.
  The services evaluate formulas only; the guest validates the inputs.
